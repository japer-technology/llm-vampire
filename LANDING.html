The ritual

How it works

A transparent proxy in front of one or more LM Studio nodes, adding opt-in orchestration without breaking a single existing client.

Wake

The vampire wakes on the local network and scans for LM Studio-compatible inference endpoints.

Verify

Each node's models, capabilities, owner tokens, and policy are checked before it joins the registry.

Route

Requests are routed by model and load, coalesced, cached, and failed-over across nodes.

Fuse

Optional fusion runs parallel, race, or judge/refiner strategies across many machines at once.

Intended experience

One command. One endpoint.

The early build steps (proxy, registry, discovery, routing, and dashboard) are runnable today; the CLI below documents the intended end-to-end experience.
