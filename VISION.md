# Vision

`llm-vampire` turns the local LLM services and computing power already around us
into one shared, private AI service.

Across homes, schools, offices, and events, LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, and
other OpenAI-compatible services often run in isolation. This project discovers
and combines that capacity safely while keeping owners in control of when and how
their services are used.

Users get simple, reliable, local AI. Owners keep control. Communities make better use of the technology they already have.
